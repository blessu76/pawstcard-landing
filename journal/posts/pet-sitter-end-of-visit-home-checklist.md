**Follow the same short route through the home before you leave each visit.** Start where the pet will stay, put away what you used, and finish with the agreed exit. A familiar order helps when you are carrying a bag, thinking about the next visit, or returning to the doorway after a distraction. Use the client's instructions as your checklist and make any exceptions visible in the visit note.

## What should you check before you head for the door?

Start with the pet's setup. Confirm where the client wants the pet to stay, which rooms should remain open, and where the water should be left. Put away food and supplies you used. If scooping the litter or taking out a tied bag is part of the visit, place the waste where the client asked. Leave toys and bedding as agreed. If something needs a refill before the next visit, write down the item and where you looked.

On your way out, look at the places your visit changed. You might have turned on a kitchen light, opened an interior door, or set a towel by the sink. Restore the agreed light and door settings. Move mail only if the client asked and leave it in the agreed spot. A porch light the owner wants on is an example of what belongs on a home-specific list.

## How do you finish at the exit and log an exception?

Check that any exterior door or gate you used is secured as agreed. Follow the client's instructions for the key, lockbox, or alarm. Keep access details out of the routine update. The [key handoff and home access checklist](/journal/en/pet-sitter-house-key-handoff-and-home-access-checklist/) has prompts for agreeing on these steps before the first visit.

Use one line to record the checks that matter for this home. If a step remains open, name it and say what you did next. For example, an included mail check can wait for the next visit if the client agrees. If an exit will not secure, contact the client promptly through the agreed channel and follow the backup plan.

> “Water topped up, litter scooped, and the porch light left on as requested. The back door is locked. I left the parcel inside by the entry.”

## What belongs on your last look?

- Verify the pet is in the agreed area with water.
- Put away supplies and leave lights, doors, and mail as agreed.
- Secure the exit and handle the key as planned.
- Record any open item and tell the client who will follow up.

# Contact

Questions, feedback, or a request about your data — write to us:

**ipe.haven@gmail.com**

## What to put in your message

- **Remove or correct your waitlist signup:** write from the email address you signed up with, and tell us what you want removed or changed. We will confirm when it is done.
- **Ask what we hold about you:** write from the same address.
- **Anything else:** just tell us what is on your mind. We read every message.

We do not offer phone or chat support. Early-access invitations are sent by email only.

More about how we handle your data is in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/). The rules for using this website are in the [Terms of Use](/terms/).

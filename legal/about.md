# About Pawstcard

Pawstcard is a report-card tool for independent pet sitters and dog walkers. The idea: snap up to three photos on a visit, tap what happened, review the note it drafts, and send your client a link — a small postcard for every visit.

**Pawstcard is in early access.** The app is not released yet. You can join the waitlist on the [home page](/#signup) and we will invite you to the beta.

## What you stay in control of

- The note is drafted from your photos and taps, and you can edit every word.
- Nothing is sent until you approve it.
- Medication entries use fixed wording instead of AI.

## The Journal

The [Journal](/journal/) is where we publish practical articles for independent pet sitters and dog walkers: contracts, invoices, visit notes and the everyday business of looking after other people's pets. It is general guidance, not professional advice — see our [Terms of Use](/terms/).

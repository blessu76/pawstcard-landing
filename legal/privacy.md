# Privacy Policy

- **Effective date:** November 7, 2026
- **Website:** https://pawstcard.ipe.rest (the "Site")
- **Operator:** ipe()
- **Contact:** ipe.haven@gmail.com

This policy covers **this website only**: the Pawstcard information page, the Journal (our articles) and the early-access waitlist. It does **not** cover the Pawstcard app. The app is not released yet; when it is, it will have its own privacy policy, which you will be able to read before you install it.

## 1. What we collect on the Site, and why

| What | When | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your email address** | You submit the waitlist form | To send you your early-access invitation | Web3Forms, then a mailbox we control |
| **Survey answers** (how you send updates to clients today; how many visits you do per day), sent together with your email address | You choose to answer the optional survey after joining | To understand what pet sitters need, and to link your answers to your signup | Web3Forms, then a mailbox we control |
| **Visit data**: pages viewed, clicks on signup buttons, starting and completing the form, referring page, browser, device, approximate location and IP address | Every visit to any page of the Site | To count visits, see which pages help, and measure ads | Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel, Cloudflare Web Analytics |
| **Technical request data** (such as IP address) | Every visit | To deliver pages and fonts | GitHub Pages (hosting) and Google Fonts |

Details:

- **Waitlist.** We use your email address to send you an early-access invitation. The form says we will send "one invite email". The email address is the only thing the form requires.
- **Survey.** The survey is optional and appears after you join. Your answers are sent with your email address so we can match them to your signup.
- **Google Analytics 4.** We record page views and four events: clicking a signup button, starting the signup form (clicking into the email box), completing signup, and completing the survey. These records do **not** contain your email address or survey answers. Google receives standard technical data from your browser.
- **Meta Pixel.** The Site loads the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel. It reports page views to Meta, and reports a "Lead" event when a signup succeeds. Meta receives technical data from your browser, such as IP address, page address and Meta cookie identifiers, and may use it to measure and improve ads and to match a visit to a Facebook or Instagram account.
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics.** The Site loads Cloudflare's analytics script to count visits and measure page performance.
- **Fonts and hosting.** Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log requests under its own policies. We run no server of our own that logs visitors.

We do not ask for payment details, passwords or other sensitive information on the Site. Please do not enter any in the survey.

## 2. Who receives your data

| Service | Receives | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com) | Email address, survey answers, technical data of the submission | Delivers form submissions to us by email |
| Google (Analytics, Fonts) | Technical data, visit events | Analytics; font delivery |
| Meta Platforms | Technical data, page views, "Lead" event | Ad measurement |
| Cloudflare | Technical data | Site analytics |
| GitHub | Technical request data | Hosting |

We do not sell your email address or survey answers.

Web3Forms forwards each submission to a mailbox we control, which only we can read.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site's own code sets no cookies and uses no local storage. The third-party tools in section 1 may set or read cookies or similar identifiers in your browser — for example Google Analytics (`_ga` cookies) and Meta Pixel (`_fbp`).

The Site currently **does not show a cookie banner and does not ask for your consent before loading analytics and the Meta Pixel**.

## 4. How long we keep data

- **Waitlist email and survey answers:** until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them to run the early-access program.
- **Visit data:** kept by Google, Meta and Cloudflare under their own settings and policies.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **Delete or correct your waitlist data:** email us at the contact address above from the address you signed up with, and we will remove it or correct it. There is no self-service page yet.
- **Ask what we hold:** email us the same way.
- **Analytics and ads:** you can block scripts and cookies with your browser or an extension, use Google's opt-out browser add-on for Google Analytics, and manage ad settings in your Facebook and Instagram accounts. The Site does not currently react to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals.
- **Your legal rights.** Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Email us to use these rights.

## 6. International transfers

Our service providers (Google, Meta, Cloudflare, GitHub, Web3Forms) are mainly based in the United States and may process data there and in other countries. By using the Site, you understand that your data may be transferred internationally.

## 7. Children

The Site is for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you think a child has joined the waitlist, contact us and we will delete the data.

## 8. Security

The Site uses HTTPS and established providers, but no system is perfectly secure.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If we change what we do with your email address in a way that matters, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.

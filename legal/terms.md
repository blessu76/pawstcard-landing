## 2. What the Service is

Pawstcard lets a pet sitter keep client and pet records on the sitter's own phone, record a visit (photos, tapped facts, optional walk route), have an AI write a draft report, review and approve it, and send a link to a web page of that report to the pet's owner. No account is required; the app signs you in anonymously only to upload what you send. Your data on the phone can be saved to a backup file you control.
